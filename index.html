Surface Science · Electrocatalysis · Nanomaterials

Hello, I’m
Lokesh Saravanan.

I am a postdoctoral researcher specializing in surface science and electrocatalysis. My research focuses on controlling the anchoring, electronic structure, and reactivity of sub-nanometer platinum clusters on modified graphitic supports.

I combine ion implantation, physical vapor deposition, scanning tunneling microscopy, X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy, and electrochemical measurements to connect atomic-scale structure with catalytic performance.

Research overview

Connecting atomic structure with electrochemical function

01

Surface Modification

Engineering graphitic surfaces through ion implantation and oxygen functionalization to create controlled anchoring sites.

02

Sub-nano Catalysts

Investigating the structure, stability, and electronic properties of size-selected platinum clusters on modified HOPG.

03

Electrocatalysis

Relating local cluster–support interactions to oxygen reduction activity and other electrochemical reactions.
